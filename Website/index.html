Welcome to Gyan Vriksh

Gyan (Knowledge) and Vriksh (Tree) grow within and around us. When the roots are nourished, the tree nurtures and flourishes all around, similarly, Gyan meaning Wisdom grows when we seek within. Gyan Vriksh acts to grow together to make our world more and more beautiful and full of happiness.

GyanVriksh is a registered non profit organization working in areas of Education, Health, Environment and General Awareness.

The organization is income tax exempted under 80(G) and 12(A) of Income tax Act 1961. Since its formation, GyanVriksh has reached more than 10 lac school children.

Our team has been working and continues to, with all sincerity, sharing best efforts in respective fields.
